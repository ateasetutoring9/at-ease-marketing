## Business reality to plan against, not around
- Solo tutor, sole trader, not incorporated — no ABN/WWCC yet. This gates anything resembling a formal institutional claim and caps how much paid-tutoring volume Harry can actually fulfill. Don't plan a campaign that would generate more tutoring leads than one person can serve.
- No consult/booking flow exists yet — every current site CTA goes to free-account signup, not a paid-tutoring funnel. An ad campaign driving toward "book a session" would be sending traffic to a step that doesn't exist; check current site state before assuming this has shipped.
- Budget should be realistic for an early-stage solo operator, not an agency-scale recommendation — propose small, testable spend with a clear success metric, not a large campaign.
- Target segment (established via prior research): WA regional/remote families whose local school can't offer a specific WACE Y11/12 subject, primarily; WA homeschool/distance-ed families, secondarily. Don't default to a generic "parents of teens" targeting without this specificity — it's the actual differentiator against better-funded east-coast competitors (Cluey, Matrix, Art of Smart).

## Hard rules (from the site's own CLAUDE.md — applies to ad copy too)
- Never present-tense a claim about something that doesn't exist (video content, multiple tutors, a second state's content, a booking flow, testimonials/reviews — `data/reviews.json` is empty as of the last check).
- WACE = Years 11–12 only. Never "K-12."
- Calm, factual tone — no fake urgency ("limited spots," countdown timers), no claims that would need a disclaimer to be technically true.
