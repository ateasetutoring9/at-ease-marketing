## 1. ICPA WA (Isolated Children's Parents' Association)

**Subject:** A free WA-curriculum resource for families without local subject access

Hi,

I'm Harry, a WA tutor, and I've built a free website — At Ease Tutoring — with
lessons and auto-graded worksheets for Years 7–12, written against the actual
WA/SCSA curriculum. No sign-up needed to browse.

I built it with exactly the problem ICPA families deal with in mind: a school
can lose a subject in a given year simply because it can't staff it, regardless
of how good a student is or how much they want to take it. This is a free second
way into that content when the local timetable doesn't have it.

There's a page specifically for this: ateasetutoring.com/distance-education/

If it'd be useful to mention in a newsletter or pass along to branches, I'm
happy to provide more detail or a short write-up. No cost, no catch.
