# Facebook teaser clips — plan

Source videos (from `lib/constants.ts`):
- Onboarding: "Creating a free At Ease Tutoring account" (~1 min) — hold back for later
- Platform tour: "Touring the At Ease Tutoring platform" — hold back for later
- Parent linking: "Linking a parent account on At Ease Tutoring" (~1 min) — **cut this one first**

Why parent-linking first: this audience (P&C/parent FB groups) responds to "I can actually
see what my kid's doing," not a generic app demo. Onboarding/tour are follow-ups once this
format proves it gets any traction.

## Steps

1. **Scan the parent-linking video first** for the old wrong product name ("At Ease
   Learning") briefly flashing on screen — known issue in 2 of the 3 videos. Cut around it
   if present; don't ship a teaser with the wrong name in it.
2. **Cut in Clipchamp** (free, built into Windows 11): trim to the parent-logs-in →
   sees-kid's-progress beat, reframe to vertical 9:16, auto-caption + proofread, keep it
   30–45 sec (Facebook autoplays muted, so captions matter more than narration).
3. **Make a Bitly short link per group/variant**, all pointing at `ateasetutoring.com`
   (or `/distance-education/` for the SIDE/distance-ed variant) — lets you see which
   group/wording actually works without adding any tracking to the site itself.
4. **Pick 2–3 groups to start, not a blast.** Check each group's posting rules first;
   message the admin if approval is required, and be upfront that you built the site —
   don't disguise it as organic parent content.
5. **Post, then wait ~1 week** before expanding. Check Bitly click counts per link before
   scaling to more groups or cutting the next clip.

Organic only — no boosting. (Past incident: boosted posts on the Page previously carried
false claims — "PERTH · MELBOURNE" service area, a fake diagnostic-session flow, subjects
not actually offered, language implying multiple tutors. Removed, but the lesson is every
claim in a post needs checking against the real site before it's drafted.)

**Regional P&C / general WA parent group:**

> curriculum, and parents can see exactly what their kid's been doing — not just a grade,
> the real activity. Quick look at what that actually looks like. Completely free, link
> below if it's useful to anyone: [link]

**Distance education / SIDE-parent group** (ties into the `/distance-education/` page):
> If your child is in distance ed and you've ever wished for a second way into a subject
> that isn't landing — this is a free library of WA-curriculum lessons and worksheets,
> separate from any enrolment, that you can use alongside it. Here's the parent side of
> it — seeing what they're actually working on. Free, no sign-up to browse: [link]

**FIFO Family Project — style group:**
> Built this partly with FIFO families in mind — a free way to actually see what your kid's
> been studying while you're away, not just wait for a report card. 30 seconds of what that
> looks like from the parent side. Free to use: [link]

All three: no scarcity language, no claim of multiple tutors, no "K-12," states plainly
that Harry built it. Adjust wording per the actual group once picked.

## Tracking (no site changes — just for personal reference)

Keep a short log: group name, which link/caption variant, date posted, Bitly click count
after 1 week. That's enough signal to decide whether to scale up or move to the next clip.
